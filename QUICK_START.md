# 🚀 MoodBeats Quick Start Guide

Get started with MoodBeats in just 5 minutes!

---

## 🎯 What is MoodBeats?

MoodBeats is a premium study productivity platform that helps you:
- **Track your focus** during study sessions
- **Discover which sounds** enhance your concentration
- **Monitor your mood** before and after studying
- **Visualize your progress** with beautiful analytics

All data stays private on your device. No signup required!

---

## 📱 Step 1: Open the App

Simply open `index.html` in your web browser. That's it!

**Supported Browsers:**
- ✅ Chrome/Edge (recommended)
- ✅ Firefox
- ✅ Safari

---

## 👤 Step 2: Set Your Name (Optional)

1. Click **"Profile"** in the sidebar
2. Enter your name in the text field
3. Your personalized greeting will appear on the Dashboard!

---

## 🎯 Step 3: Set Your Weekly Goal

1. Stay on the **Profile** page
2. Adjust the "Weekly Study Goal" slider
3. Recommended: 150 minutes/week for beginners
4. Your goal appears on the Dashboard

---

## 🎵 Step 4: Start Your First Session

### Quick Method (from Dashboard):
1. Go to **Dashboard**
2. Click the big **"New Study Session"** button

### Detailed Method (from Study page):
1. Click **"Study"** in the sidebar
2. You'll see a setup form with 4 sections:

#### 📝 Section 1: How are you feeling?
- Select your current mood (😞 to 😄)
- Set your stress level (1-10)
- Set your focus level (1-10)

#### 📚 Section 2: What are you studying?
- Choose your task: Reading, Writing, Problem solving, etc.

#### 🎧 Section 3: Choose your audio
- Pick a sound type:
  - **Lo-fi** 🎵 - Mellow chords (great for general study)
  - **Instrumental** 🎹 - Soft arpeggios (for creative work)
  - **Nature** 🌿 - Rain and wind (very calming)
  - **Frequency** 🎧 - Binaural beats (use headphones!)
  - **Ambient** 🌌 - Atmospheric sounds (for deep work)
  - **Silence** 🔇 - No sound (for complete quiet)

#### ⏱️ Section 4: Session duration
- Choose: 1 min (test), 15, 25, 45, or 60 minutes
- **Tip**: Start with 25 minutes (Pomodoro-style)

3. Click **"Start Session"** 🎯

---

## ⏰ Step 5: Study with the Timer

Once started, you'll see:
- 🎵 A beautiful spinning vinyl record
- ⏱️ A countdown timer
- 🔊 Volume control (if audio is playing)

**Controls:**
- **Pause** ⏸️ - Take a break (timer and audio pause)
- **Resume** ▶️ - Continue your session
- **End and Check In** ⏹️ - Finish early

**Pro Tip**: Let the full timer complete for best tracking!

---

## ✅ Step 6: Complete the Check-in

After your session:

1. **Rate how you feel NOW:**
   - Select your current mood
   - Set your current stress level
   - Set your current focus level

2. Click **"Save Session"** 💾

3. See your results! The app shows:
   - Focus change (how much your focus improved)
   - Stress change (how much your stress reduced)
   - Mood change (how much your mood improved)

**Remember**: One session is just one data point. Patterns emerge after several sessions!

---

## 📊 Step 7: Track Your Progress

### Dashboard View

- Check your **weekly goal progress** 🎯

- Explore **study insights** 📈

### Sessions View

## 📈 Understanding Your Data

### Positive vs Negative Changes

**Focus Change**:
- **Positive (+)** = Your focus improved ✅
- **Negative (-)** = Your focus decreased ❌

**Stress Change**:
- **Positive (+)** = Your stress REDUCED ✅ (pre > post)
- **Negative (-)** = Your stress increased ❌

**Mood Change**:
- **Positive (+)** = Your mood improved ✅
- **Negative (-)** = Your mood decreased ❌

### What Do the Colors Mean?

- 🟢 **Green** = Positive change (good!)
- 🔴 **Red** = Negative change (not ideal)
- ⚪ **Gray** = No change

## 🤔 Common Questions

### Q: Do I need to create an account?
**A**: No! Everything is stored locally in your browser.

### Q: Is my data sent to a server?
**A**: No! All data stays on your device. Complete privacy.

### Q: Can I use this on mobile?
**A**: Yes! The design is responsive and works on all devices.

### Q: Do I need headphones?
**A**: Only for "Frequency" audio (binaural beats). Others work through speakers.

### Q: Can I upload my own music?
**A**: Not yet. All audio is synthetically generated for consistency.

### Q: How many sessions should I do?
**A**: At least 3-5 to start seeing patterns. The more data, the better!

### Q: What if I need to stop mid-session?
**A**: Click "End and Check In" to save your partial session.

### Q: Can I change my goal later?
**A**: Yes! Go to Profile and adjust the slider anytime.

### Q: Will I lose data if I close the browser?
**A**: No! Data persists using localStorage.

### Q: What if I clear my browser data?
**A**: You'll lose all sessions. Export isn't available yet, so be careful!

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Audio not playing?
- Check browser permissions
- Unmute your tab
- Adjust volume slider
- Try a different browser

### Timer not starting?
- Refresh the page
- Check JavaScript is enabled
- Clear browser cache

### Data not saving?
- Check localStorage is enabled
- Check available storage space
- Try a different browser

### Charts not showing?
- Complete at least 3 sessions
- Refresh the page
- Check browser console for errors
